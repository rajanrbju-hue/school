About Our School

Welcome to Holy Angels School, Nagara

Situated on the Chhapra-Mohammadpur State Highway (SH-90) in Nagara, Saran (Bihar), Holy Angels School is committed to delivering quality general school education with character building and academic excellence.

Humare school me bacho ke sarvangeen vikas (holistic development) par vishesh dhyan diya jata hai. Modern teaching aids, disciplined environment, aur experienced teachers ke sath hum bacho ko aage badhne ka behtar avsar pradan karte hain.

  • CBSE curriculum based holistic study plan
  • Qualified and supportive teaching staff
  • Emphasis on moral values, sports & cultural activities
  • Affordable fee structure for parents in Saran district
